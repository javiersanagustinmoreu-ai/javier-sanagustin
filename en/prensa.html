Exhibition

Reflejos y sombras (Reflections and Shadows), first solo exhibition by Javier Sanagustín Moreu.

Palacio de Villahermosa, Fundación Ibercaja, Huesca.
8 October – 11 November 2026.

Fourteen works that come from an approach to the history of painting, out of the need to recognise oneself in what others had already painted.

What came out recognisable is a reflection. What came out transformed, displaced, is a shadow.

Short biography

Javier Sanagustín Moreu (Huesca, 1995) is a painter and director of the Escuela de Dibujo y Pintura Sanagustín. His training lies outside the academy: he trained in the classroom, teaching and watching his students work. His work evolves from realism toward a gestural language, where stain and water carry the emotion of each painting.

Q&A

Translated from the Spanish. Translation pending the artist’s review.

Where does this exhibition come from?
It comes from a visit to the Prado. I heard a boy say to his father, let’s go and see Goya. He said it like someone going to see somebody close, somebody who is still there.

What do you hope visitors take away?
That they stop for a moment in front of each work. I don’t paint so that people understand my ideas. I paint so that each person finds their own.

You make your debut at the Palacio de Villahermosa. What does that mean?
I feel privileged. I always wanted my first exhibition to be here, in Huesca, because the landscapes are from here, my surroundings are this, my people are here.
