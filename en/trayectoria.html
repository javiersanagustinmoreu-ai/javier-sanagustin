Career

Javier Sanagustín Moreu

Born in Huesca in 1995. He grows up in the «Escuela de Dibujo y Pintura Sanagustín», founded by his parents, and begins teaching there at 16. He has directed the school since 2019.

His training lies outside the academy. His painting is formed in the classroom, teaching, watching his students work and the painters they point out to him.

His work evolves from realism toward a gestural language, where stain and water carry the emotion of each painting.

«Reflejos y sombras» (Reflections and Shadows) is his first solo exhibition.

Artist statement

Translated from the Spanish text on the exhibition website. Translation pending the artist’s review.

This exhibition comes from an experience at the Prado Museum. I heard a boy say to his father, “Dad, let’s go and see Goya.” I felt it like someone who wants to see somebody close to them. Somebody who is here.

And I had lived it before. Something in those painters pulled me toward them without my knowing why. Now I know it was not learning. It was recognition. Something in them was already in me before I found them.

That is why I paint with them. Not from the distance of someone who studies, but as someone who paints with a fluid, stained and emotional gesture. I do not mean to make versions or homages. They are the record of what happened when that work passed through me.

Pain, laughter, sadness. We will never know whether your tears taste like mine. I can only sense the strength of your hands, hear the weeping of your silence.

That is why I paint. Not so that you feel what I feel. So that you find what you already carried inside, and reflect your feelings through a language that belongs only to you.

I looked for you without knowing it. And I found myself.

I put in mine. You put in yours. And in that crossing, something happens that neither of us could have created alone. What you will see is not me, nor is it you.

It is my shadow in the reflection of your feelings.
