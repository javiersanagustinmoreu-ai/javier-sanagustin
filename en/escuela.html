The school

Escuela de Dibujo y Pintura Sanagustín

It is a creative space in the old town of Huesca. It was founded in 1994 by Javier’s parents, among them the city painter Fernando Sanagustín. Today it is run by his son Javier Sanagustín, who has kept the school’s original spirit: a creative and welcoming place for those who are passionate about art.

Anyone, whatever their age or level of experience, can come to paint and discover their creativity in a relaxed atmosphere. In class we adapt to each person’s needs, from those who are just starting to draw and paint to the most experienced.

Work is done in small groups by age and level, with techniques ranging from oil to watercolour, taking in acrylic, graphite and wax crayons, with professional materials to try them all. There is plenty of room and an outdoor space for sunny days.

Javier grew up surrounded by palettes, canvases and brushes. As a child he watched his father paint and waited to turn six so he could be a pupil like the others. Today his passion is his profession, and his classes pass on his enthusiasm for art.
