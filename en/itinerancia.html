Touring

Reflejos y sombras (Reflections and Shadows) is looking for venues in 2027.

It is the first solo exhibition by Javier Sanagustín Moreu. Fourteen works, thirteen oil paintings and one in mixed media, painted between 2021 and 2026. It was shown at the Palacio de Villahermosa, Fundación Ibercaja, Huesca, from 8 October to 11 November 2026, as part of the programme for the Foundation’s 150th anniversary.

What it includes

Fourteen works in five sections: opening, landscape block, hinge, figure block and closing. Five are reflections and nine are shadows.

Gallery texts. Each work has a text, its process and its reference, read through a QR code in the room. The gallery texts are in Spanish; each one is also available on this site in English.

Catalogue. First edition, 2026. ISBN 978-84-09-90620-8. Legal deposit HU 214-2026. €20.

Performance. Participatory, in three stations: the shadow, the reflection and a written question. The result stays in the room.

Workshop for school groups, led by the artist, ages 6 to 13, up to 25 pupils, 45 to 60 minutes.

Space

The fourteen works add up to about 19 linear metres of wall, not counting the space between them. The widest piece is Entre fragmentos, a polyptych 348 cm wide. The tallest is Sin distancia, 195 cm.

Terms

Loan, transport, installation and insurance, to be agreed with each venue. Write with the available dates and the dimensions of the space.
