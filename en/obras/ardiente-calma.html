Ardiente calma

Oil on canvas, 120 × 120 cm, 2024.

The calm sea in the background, the open sky, the most vibrant colour of summer flooding everything. I had to find someone who could hold that light. Not just anyone. Someone in whom the sun would want to stay.

Water seeps in, covers, advances over the dress. Calm is a state crossed by something that does not ask permission. The oleander leaves us in doubt. Will she wake?

Reference: Frederic Leighton, Flaming June
