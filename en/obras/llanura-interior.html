Llanura interior

Oil on canvas, 114 × 195 cm, 2025.

This landscape does not exist. It is fragments, impossible distances, memories that have forgotten where they came from. There is no exact point from which to look, because what is sought is not a place but the image that remains once memory has finished its work.

Seas of ochre broken by green islets. Low hills (tozales) that emerge without fuss. A holm oak in the centre that is not presence but insistence, a point where something of one’s own becomes recognisable. Earth green turning to yellow. Aragon.

And always, winding through it, a line that leads the eye to where everything ends, the sierra. Too present. As happens in memory when something becomes inevitable.

Reference: Claire Tabouret, Grande Calme
