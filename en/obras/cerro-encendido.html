Cerro encendido

Oil on canvas, 100 × 70 cm, 2025.

An afternoon leaving the Alcoraz stadium. The hill of San Jorge, with the light setting everything alight. Not burning, but just as violent. Orange tinting the trees from within, as if the light came from inside the hill.

The contrast demands it. The cold, dense blue of the forest leaves the orange of the branches no choice but to cry out.

Reference: Piet Mondrian, The Red Tree
