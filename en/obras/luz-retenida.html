Luz retenida

Oil on canvas, 120 × 110 cm, 2025.

A cold morning. An ordinary walk through the park, with the nearby buildings blocking the light. In that half-light, beams of light slipped past the obstacles and revealed small patches of colour; they did not warm, but they showed.

That is how it appeared: a red tulip, single, awake among fine lines of green and blue, among companions still dimmed.

The reality that mattered was not botanical. It was pictorial.

Reference: Claude Monet, Agapanthus
