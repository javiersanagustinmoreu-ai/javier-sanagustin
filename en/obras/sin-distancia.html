Sin distancia

Oil on canvas, 195 × 186 cm, 2023.

Five figures impose themselves, without offering themselves, without arranging themselves for anyone.

The space has no way out. The figures, the background, the curtains, everything compresses until there is no distance between the image and whoever looks at it. The painting obliges you to stay.

Reference: Pablo Picasso, Les Demoiselles d’Avignon
