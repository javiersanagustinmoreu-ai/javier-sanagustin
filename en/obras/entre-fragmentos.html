Entre fragmentos

Oil on canvas, polyptych, 348 × 178 cm, 2026.

This willow has stood in the Parque de las Olas for years. A living architecture that grows until it lets itself fall.

And a small glance was not enough.

There is something here that only happens when you stand at its feet. Not seeing it from afar, not in a photograph. At its feet. A warmth that does not come from the tree but from within you, that rises slowly, that weighs. Where the branches fall toward you, covering you.

Reference: David Hockney, Woldgate Woods
