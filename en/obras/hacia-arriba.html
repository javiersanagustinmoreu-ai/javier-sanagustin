Hacia arriba

Oil on panel, Ø 80 cm, 2022.

Sold.

To look up. I wanted to walk and look the way Van Gogh looked at his almond trees in bloom. Not at the tree, from inside the tree.

The format widens that gaze the way the eye would, without corners, without limits, without a horizon. Only sky and branches. Two trees. Not parting. I did not know whether they were trying to touch or to avoid each other. But there they were, in the centre, with that tension that does not resolve. And only the wind dares to bring them together.

Reference: Vincent van Gogh, Almond Blossom
